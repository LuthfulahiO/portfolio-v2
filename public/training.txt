# Personal Essay for School Applications

Hult Business School Personal Essay
I have been working as a software engineer for the past five years, successfully building
and scaling global products. However, I have come to realize that I need to acquire
more business skills to reach my full potential. Even though I have interesting ideas and
problems to solve, I struggle to find the right business fit for them.
Two years ago, I moved to the UAE to create a peer-to-peer errand solution for the
expatriate population. I focused on building the best technology use case, but my team
lacked the business acumen. At a pitch competition in Dubai, an investor asked about
our revenue plan, leaving me embarrassed and realizing my shortcomings.
Since then, I have shifted my focus to product development and gained some
understanding of business management. T o address my knowledge gap, I want to
pursue a business degree at Hult International Business School.
After my diploma studies, I did not pursue an actual degree because the institutions
available to me at that time were not designed for builders like me. Earlier in life, I had
always wanted to be a builder, so I took an interest in software engineering. However,
recently a colleague of mine announced that he was starting his MBA at Hult Business
School. This was the first time I had heard of the school, and the phrase "The Business
School for Those Made to Do" caught my attention. This phrase resonated well with me
at the particular phase of my life when I saw it, which was the same time I realized I had
a knowledge gap preventing me from "doing". Seeing a school specifically made for that
made it a solid choice for me.
I spent the next couple of days researching the school and found that everything about
it seemed just right, from practical focused learning to career-oriented programs, and
most importantly, an immersive learning experience that simulates real-life workspace. I
concluded that pursuing an undergraduate degree at Hult was the right choice for me in
advancing my career, which is why I have decided to apply.
Learning software engineering through online courses was relatively easy, but I
recognize the different nature of the business world. Watching YouTube videos and
taking online courses without a degree won’t suffice. I noticed that top business leaders
possess degrees or MBAs, which I currently need. This realization has motivated me to
embrace the challenge of entering the corporate world. Going back to school was a
difficult decision, but it is necessary for my plans. Studying here will provide me with
global skills, network, experience and education.
In summary, I am excited to take this step and overcome the challenges that come with
it, as I strive to solve global problems and achieve my goals.
